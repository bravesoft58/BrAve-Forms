### 4. Nevada Dust Control Log (ADDITIONAL TEMPLATE - VALIDATED)

**User Request:** Research dust control log used in Nevada construction

**Research Date:** 2025-10-23 (Additional research)

**Industry Standard:** Clark County Air Quality Regulations Section 94, Nevada DEP Surface Area Disturbance (SAD) Permits

**VALIDATED: Template #11 - Dust Control Daily Log**

---

#### Regulatory Context

**Nevada Jurisdiction-Specific Requirements:**

- **Clark County (Las Vegas area):** Dust Control Operating Permit (DCOP) required for:
  - Soil disturbing activities ≥0.25 acres
  - Mechanized trenching ≥100 feet
  - Mechanical demolition ≥1,000 sq ft
  - Contact: Clark County Div. of Air Quality 702-455-1524

- **Nevada DEP (Outside Clark/Washoe):** Surface Area Disturbance (SAD) Permit required for:
  - Soil disturbing activities ≥5 acres
  - Contact: NDEP 775-687-9349

- **Washoe County (Reno area):** Dust Control Permit required for:
  - Soil disturbing activities ≥1 acre
  - Contact: Northern Nevada Public Health 775-784-7200

**Key Regulation:** Clark County Air Quality Regulations Section 94 - Permitting and Dust Control for Construction Activities

**Dust Control Monitor Required:** Projects with ≥50 acres of actively disturbed soil
