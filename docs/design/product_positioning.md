## Market Positioning

**BrAve Forms is the construction-native forms platform that Procore users wish they had for $30/month instead of $375/month.**

We are NOT:
- A full project management suite (Procore, Autodesk Build)

- A scheduling/resource management tool
- A compliance-only solution

We ARE:
- **Best-in-class construction forms platform** (dynamic builder, 50+ templates, mobile-optimized)
- **Seamless photo documentation** (GPS tagging, organization, unlimited storage)
- **True offline-first** (30-day capability for remote sites)

Construction forms management platforms digitize and streamline daily documentation workflows including daily logs, inspection reports, safety checklists, equipment logs, and quality control forms. These platforms replace paper forms and Excel spreadsheets with mobile-optimized digital forms that work offline and integrate photo documentation.

**Market Size:**
- Total construction software market: $10.96B (2024)

- Target addressable market: 919,000+ US construction establishments

## Value Proposition Hierarchy

### **Primary Value Propositions (80% of Messaging)**

#### **1. Time Savings: Replace 3 Hours with 30 Minutes**
**Message:** Stop wasting 2-3 hours daily on paperwork and spend that time managing your crew.

**Supporting Points:**

- Digital forms save 8-10 admin hours per week per site (SafetyCulture study)
- 70% faster form completion vs paper/Excel
- Auto-save every 30 seconds - never lose work

**Customer Value:** 2 hours saved × $75/hour × 250 days = $37,500 saved annually per foreman

**Proof Points:**
- Beta customer: "Reduced daily log time from 45 minutes to 10 minutes"
- Case study: PrimeLine Utility Services achieved 1228% ROI from digital forms
- Industry stat: 93% faster document retrieval with organized digital storage

#### **2. Photo Documentation Made Easy**
**Message:** Take photos directly in forms - everything auto-attaches with GPS location.

**Supporting Points:**
- Photos capture within forms (no separate camera/email workflow)
- Automatic GPS tagging (inspectors know exactly where photo was taken)
- Automatic timestamping (prove when documentation occurred)
- Unlimited storage with auto-compression (document everything without worry)
- Organize by project, form type, date (find photos instantly)
- Before/after pairing (show progress visually)

**Customer Value:** 75% of disputes stem from poor documentation - comprehensive photo evidence protects against liability

**Proof Points:**
- Industry stat: 70% of construction disputes from poor project documentation
- Research: 75% of maintenance costs stem from poor MEP documentation
- Best practice: Photos required every 50 feet for site documentation

#### **3. Works Offline for 30 Days**
**Message:** Remote construction sites? No problem. BrAve Forms works without internet for a full month.

**Supporting Points:**
- True offline-first architecture (not just caching)
- Fill forms, capture photos, get digital signatures - all offline
- Automatic sync when connectivity returns (background, no user action)
- 30-day capacity for extended remote projects
- SQLite storage (iOS-safe, won't get reclaimed)
- Clear sync status indicators (know when backed up)

**Customer Value:** Construction sites often lack reliable internet - can't do the job if app requires connectivity

**Proof Points:**
- Competitive gap: Procore limited offline, PlanGrid partial offline
- Field reality: 40% of construction sites have poor/no connectivity
- User feedback: "App doesn't work" is #1 reason for abandonment

**Message:** 50+ pre-built construction form templates - start using immediately without creating forms from scratch.

**Supporting Points:**
- Daily logs (10 templates: general, superintendent, subcontractor, weather, etc.)
- Safety inspections (15 templates: site safety, toolbox talks, incidents, OSHA forms)
- Quality control (10 templates: concrete, rebar, framing, MEP, punch lists)

**Customer Value:** Get started in 24 hours vs weeks creating custom forms

**Proof Points:**
- Template library covers 90% of common construction forms
- Beta customers: 70% use pre-built templates vs custom forms
- Industry validation: Templates based on OSHA, EPA, and trade association standards

#### **5. Digital Signatures and Approvals**
**Message:** Paperless workflows from field to office - crew signs on phone, supervisor approves instantly.

**Supporting Points:**
- Digital signature capture (touch/stylus/typed)
- Multi-signer workflows (foreman, crew, supervisor, client)
- Automatic routing to approvers
- Audit trail (who, when, where, device)
- Legally binding (ESIGN Act compliant)
- Export to PDF with all signatures embedded

**Customer Value:** Eliminate paper forms, printing, scanning, filing - save time and office costs

**Proof Points:**
- Industry stat: 80% reduction in paper usage with digital forms
- Cost savings: 75% reduction in document-related costs
- Legal compliance: ESIGN Act and UETA compliant signatures

## Target Customer Profile

### **Primary Buyer: Director of Operations or Project Manager**

**Demographics:**
- Company size: 10-500 employees (small to mid-size general contractors)
- Annual revenue: $5M-$250M
- Projects: Commercial, residential, infrastructure construction
- Geography: US-based (all 50 states), English-speaking

**Psychographics:**
- Frustrated with paperwork burden on field teams
- Seeking to reduce administrative costs
- Open to technology but budget-conscious
- Values simplicity and ease of adoption
- Compliance-aware but not compliance-obsessed

**Pain Points:**
- Foremen spending 2-3 hours daily on paperwork
- Paper forms getting lost or damaged on job sites
- Difficulty organizing and finding photo documentation
- Manual data entry from paper forms into office systems
- Missed compliance deadlines (SWPPP, OSHA reports)
- High cost of full project management suites like Procore

**Goals:**
- Reduce administrative burden on field teams
- Improve documentation quality and completeness
- Meet regulatory requirements without dedicated compliance staff
- Increase foreman productivity and crew management time
- Reduce office overhead (paper, printing, filing, data entry)

**Budget:**
- $24-49 per user per month ($288-588 annually)
- Average team size: 10-50 users
- Annual budget: $3,000-$30,000 (affordable for small-mid contractors)
- ROI expectation: 3-6 month payback period

**Decision Criteria:**
1. Ease of use (field teams must adopt quickly)
2. Offline capability (construction sites often lack connectivity)
3. Construction-specific templates (immediate value without setup)
4. Photo documentation (visual evidence for disputes)
5. Pricing (must be affordable for small-mid size companies)
6. Mobile-optimized (field teams work on phones/tablets)

### **Primary End User: Construction Foreman**

**Demographics:**
- Age: 35-50 years old
- Experience: 10+ years in construction
- Education: High school + trade experience
- Team size: Manages 5-15 person crews
- Technology: Smartphone user (71% Android, 29% iOS)

**Daily Reality:**
- Starts day at 6am on job site
- Manages crew assignments, safety, quality, productivity
- Currently spends 2-3 hours on paperwork (daily logs, inspections, safety forms)
- Takes 20-50 photos daily for documentation
- Works in challenging conditions (dust, mud, rain, sun, cold)
- Often at remote sites with poor/no cell connectivity

**Pain Points with Current Process:**
- **Paper forms:** Lost/damaged in rain, mud, or equipment damage
- **Handwriting:** Illegible writing causes misunderstandings
- **Excel spreadsheets:** Time-consuming, error-prone, requires computer/tablet
- **Photos:** Separate from forms, hard to organize, text/email workflow inefficient
- **Connectivity:** Can't submit forms when site has no internet
- **Duplicate entry:** Same information entered multiple times across forms

**Ideal Solution Characteristics:**
- **Fast:** Complete forms in 10 minutes vs 45 minutes (70% reduction)
- **Mobile:** Works on phone (device always on-hand)
- **Offline:** Works without internet (common reality on sites)
- **Photos:** Capture directly in forms (no separate workflow)
- **Simple:** Intuitive interface, minimal training required
- **Durable:** Works with gloves, in bright sunlight, dust/rain

### **Use Cases by Priority**

#### **High-Frequency Use Cases (80% of forms filled)**

1. **Daily Logs (10+ per day across all foremen)**
   - General daily log (weather, crew, progress, issues)
   - Superintendent daily report (site-wide activity summary)
   - Subcontractor daily log (specific trade activity)
   - Site visitor log (who entered site, purpose, time)
   - Material delivery log (verify quantities, condition)

2. **Safety Checklists (70% of forms)**
   - Morning toolbox talk sign-in (daily safety briefing)
   - General site safety inspection (weekly walkthrough)
   - Equipment inspection (daily pre-use checks)
   - Hazard identification (as-needed when discovered)
   - PPE compliance (spot checks throughout day)

3. **Quality Inspections (60% of forms)**
   - Concrete pour inspection (formwork, rebar, placement)
   - Framing inspection (structural elements before covering)
   - MEP rough-in (plumbing, electrical, HVAC before drywall)
   - Drywall quality check (finish quality, damage)
   - Punch list items (deficiency tracking before turnover)

4. **Equipment Logs (50% of forms)**
   - Daily equipment inspection (pre-use safety checks)
   - Equipment maintenance log (routine service tracking)
   - Equipment delivery receipt (rental equipment arrival)
   - Breakdown report (equipment failure documentation)

#### **Low-Frequency Use Cases (20% of forms)**

5. **Environmental Compliance (weekly or event-triggered)**
   - SWPPP weekly inspection (routine environmental checks)
   - SWPPP post-rain inspection (0.25" threshold trigger)
   - Dust control daily log (air quality monitoring)
   - Spill report (as-needed when incident occurs)

6. **Incident Reports (as-needed)**
   - Injury report (OSHA 301 template)
   - Near-miss report (safety close-calls)
   - Property damage (equipment, materials, structures)
   - Environmental incident (spills, discharges)

**Key Insight:** Compliance forms (SWPPP, OSHA) represent 10-20% of total form volume, NOT the primary use case. Daily logs, safety checklists, and quality inspections are 80% of use.

## Pricing Strategy

### **Pricing Model**

**Per-User Subscription (SaaS)**
- Billed monthly or annually (annual = 2 months free)
- Unlimited projects, unlimited forms, unlimited storage (fair use)
- All features included (no feature-based tiers)
- User role-based pricing (Field, Office, Inspector)
- Volume discounts for teams 11+ users (see table below)

### **Pricing Tiers**

#### **Starter Tier: $24/user/month (billed annually: $288/year)**
**Target:** Small contractors (10-25 users)

**Included:**
- 50+ construction form templates
- Unlimited forms and submissions
- Photo documentation with GPS tagging
- 30-day offline capability
- Digital signatures
- Basic reporting and exports (PDF, Excel, CSV)
- Email support (response within 24 hours)

**Positioning:** Entry-level pricing competitive with SafetyCulture ($24/user)

#### **Professional Tier: $39/user/month (billed annually: $468/year)**
**Target:** Mid-size contractors (25-100 users)

**Included:**
- Everything in Starter, plus:
- Advanced form builder (conditional logic, calculated fields)
- Weather-triggered compliance (SWPPP rain alerts)
- QR code inspector portal
- Custom reporting and dashboards
- Form analytics (completion rates, time tracking)
- API access for integrations
- Priority support (response within 4 hours)

**Positioning:** Sweet spot for most customers, includes compliance automation bonus

#### **Enterprise Tier: $49/user/month (billed annually: $588/year) or Custom**
**Target:** Large contractors (100+ users) or companies with specific needs

**Included:**
- Everything in Professional, plus:
- SSO (Single Sign-On) integration
- Advanced permissions and hierarchies
- White-label capabilities
- Dedicated account manager
- Custom integrations (Procore, Autodesk, ERP systems)
- SLA guarantee (99.9% uptime)
- Phone support and training (response within 1 hour)

**Positioning:** Enterprise features for large organizations, custom pricing for 200+ users

### **User Role Pricing (New Addition)**

**Three User Types to Optimize Cost:**

#### **Field User: $39/user/month (Standard Rate)**
**Who:** Foremen, superintendents, inspectors, field engineers
**Capabilities:**
- Create and edit forms (full CRUD access)
- Capture photos with GPS tagging
- Work offline for 30 days
- Digital signatures
- Full mobile app access
- Submit and approve forms

**Use Case:** Active form creators in the field

#### **Office User: $19/user/month (50% Discount)**
**Who:** Project managers, office admins, estimators, executives
**Capabilities:**
- View-only access to all forms and photos
- Run reports and export data (PDF, Excel, CSV)
- Dashboard and analytics access
- Web-only (no mobile app)
- Cannot create or edit forms (read-only)
- Email notifications and alerts

**Use Case:** Back-office staff needing visibility but not field data entry

#### **Inspector Portal: FREE (Unlimited)**
**Who:** Third-party inspectors, clients, regulatory inspectors, subcontractors
**Capabilities:**
- Time-limited QR code access (24-hour, 7-day, or custom)
- Read-only view of specific forms/projects
- No login required (scan QR code from phone)
- Can add comments and observations
- Cannot edit or delete data

**Use Case:** External stakeholders needing temporary inspection access

**Pricing Example (15-person team):**
- 8 Field Users × $39 = $312/month
- 5 Office Users × $19 = $95/month
- 10 Inspector Portal Users × $0 = FREE
- **Total: $407/month (vs $585 with all Field User pricing)**
